# Code of conduct

This project is for anyone who wants to use or improve the audit module. We expect people here to be respectful, to assume good faith, and to keep the conversation on the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, including with people who are new to the project.
- Disagree about the code, the model, or the design. Do not attack the person.
- Accept that a review can ask for changes.
- Keep private information private. Do not post secrets, tokens, credentials, or someone else's private data.

## Behaviour that is not welcome

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or sexual language aimed at a person.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spamming issues or pull requests, or using them to push malware or scams.

## Where this applies

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space maintained for this project.

## Reporting

If someone is breaking this code of conduct, contact the maintainers through a GitHub issue, or through [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/AmbitiousBots/ambitious-audit/security/advisories/new) when the report itself should not be public. The maintainers of Ambitious will look at the report and may warn the person, or remove their comments, or block them from the repository.

Reports are handled by the people who maintain this repository. They will reply, and they will keep the report as private as the situation allows.
